Trip plan app

North Wales

Friday 6 to Monday 9 November, three nights at The Cherries

The trip plan as an app. Each day’s stops with parking, food and toilets, halal food first. Food shops that tell you if they’re open right now, and 28 places to visit, closest first.

Download the Android app APK, about 4 MB

On Android, open the file and allow installs from your browser when asked. On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Both work with no signal once opened.

Open it on your phone Scan with the camera, or go to anikeee.github.io/north-wales-trip
